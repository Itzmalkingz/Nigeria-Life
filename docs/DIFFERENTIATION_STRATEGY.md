# Nigeria Life — Differentiation Strategy

**Purpose:** Define how Nigeria Life stands apart from Lagos Life and other life sims while honoring our vision: *"One beginning. Many choices. One life."*

---

## 1. Core Differentiation Pillars

| Pillar | Lagos Life | Nigeria Life | Why It Matters |
|--------|------------|--------------|----------------|
| **Narrative Architecture** | Emergent-only (player stories) | **Authored life missions + legacy arcs** with branching consequences | Players remember *choices that mattered*, not just stats that grew |
| **Systemic Depth** | Linear careers, static venues | **Interlocking systems**: needs ↔ skills ↔ reputation ↔ economy ↔ events | Emergent stories from system collision, not just player chat |
| **Crime & Consequence** | Referenced, not systemic | **Fictionalized crime path** with investigation, evidence, reputation, prison | Adult theme with weight; not a clicker |
| **Relationships** | Real players only (chat, marriage) | **NPCs with memory, personality, schedules** + real players later | Single-player depth; multiplayer amplifies, doesn't replace |
| **Time & Offline** | Real-time decay, no offline protection | **Configurable offline rules**: capped decay, no rewards, fair to casual | Respects adult lives; no "punished for sleeping" |
| **Geographic Scope** | Lagos (+ Abuja/PH in code) | **Multi-city from design**: Lagos, Abuja, Port Harcourt, Kano — distinct economies | Nigeria is not just Lagos; replayability via regional starts |
| **Visual Identity** | Low-poly, emoji pins, billboards | **Branded system**: branching logo, Sora/Inter, warm ivory/dark navy, subtle path motifs | Recognizable, ownable, mature without grim |
| **Monetization** | Ads + Game Naira top-ups | **Cosmetic/QoL only**; no pay-to-win; ethical F2P | Trust > ARPU; 18+ audience values fairness |
| **Accessibility** | Keyboard shortcuts, PWA | **WCAG AA from M0**: contrast, motion, screen reader, high-contrast mode | Inclusive by default, not retrofit |

## 2. System-by-System Classification

For each major system, classify as **Genre Foundation**, **Opportunity**, or **Unique Identity**.

### 2.1 Needs & Time
| System | Classification | Nigeria Life Approach |
|--------|----------------|----------------------|
| 6 needs (hunger, energy, hygiene, bladder, fun, social) | **Genre Foundation** | Implement independently; tune decay rates for Nigerian context (e.g., heat → faster hygiene decay; traffic → energy drain) |
| Real-time decay | **Opportunity** | **Configurable time scale** (1 min = 30/60/90 game min); **offline cap** (max 8h decay); **sleep = time skip + recovery** |
| Sickness | **Genre Foundation** | Malaria, typhoid, food poisoning — treated at chemist/clinic/hospital; vaccine skills |
| NEPA/power | **Genre Foundation** | Generator/inverter/solar as housing upgrades; outage events affect activities |

### 2.2 Jobs & Career Progression
| System | Classification | Nigeria Life Approach |
|--------|----------------|----------------------|
| Linear career ladder (5 levels, skill-gated) | **Genre Foundation** | Keep as *one* path; add **apprenticeship**, **freelance**, **business ownership** as parallel tracks |
| Shift work (show up, work style choices) | **Genre Foundation** | Keep; add **performance reviews**, **office politics**, **layoff risk**, **union/collective action** |
| Side gigs | **Genre Foundation** | Expand: **hustle economy** (POS agent, ride-hail, tutoring, content, import/export) with reputation gates |
| **Business ownership** | **Unique Identity** | **Player-created businesses**: register CAC, rent shop, hire staff, manage inventory, pay taxes, compete — persistent entity outlives character |
| **Career switching** | **Opportunity** | **Transferable skills + reputation**: comms from banking → politics; coding from tech → fintech startup |

### 2.3 Housing & Assets
| System | Classification | Nigeria Life Approach |
|--------|----------------|----------------------|
| Rent tiers (Mushin → Banana Island) | **Genre Foundation** | Implement; add **lease terms**, **service charges**, **landlord disputes**, **eviction court process** |
| Buy Mode furniture | **Genre Foundation** | Keep; add **electricity-dependent items**, **status furniture** (unlocks social options) |
| **Property investment** | **Opportunity** | **Buy-to-let**: collect rent, manage tenants, omo-onile levies, maintenance, appreciation |
| **Land banking** | **Unique Identity** | **Speculative land** in developing corridors (Ibeju-Lekki, Kubwa, Igando) — risk/reward, community disputes, govt acquisition |

### 2.4 Money & Economy
| System | Classification | Nigeria Life Approach |
|--------|----------------|----------------------|
| Cash + Bank (Game Naira) | **Genre Foundation** | Separate ledgers; **POS fees**, **transfer limits**, **daily withdrawal caps**, **CBN policies as events** |
| Salary + tax | **Genre Foundation** | PAYE, VAT, withholding tax; **tax evasion path** (risk: audit, fines, prison) |
| **Investment products** | **Opportunity** | **Treasury bills, mutual funds, crypto (fictional), agric schemes, chit funds (ajo/esusu)** — each with risk curve |
| **Informal finance** | **Unique Identity** | **Esusu/ajo rotating savings** — trust-based, social enforcement, default consequences; **POS agent float management** |

### 2.5 Skills & Progression
| System | Classification | Nigeria Life Approach |
|--------|----------------|----------------------|
| 9 skills, action-based XP | **Genre Foundation** | Implement; **skill trees with specializations** (Coding → Frontend/Backend/Data/AI) |
| Traits modify growth | **Genre Foundation** | Keep; **traits also unlock unique dialogue/options** |
| **Mentorship & apprenticeship** | **Unique Identity** | **NPC mentors** teach skills faster, unlock hidden paths, require relationship investment |

### 2.6 Relationships & Social
| System | Classification | Nigeria Life Approach |
|--------|----------------|----------------------|
| Real-player chat, Padi, marriage | **Genre Foundation** | Phase 2+; design for **async first** (letters, voice notes, scheduled meetups) |
| **NPC relationships** | **Unique Identity** | **100+ NPCs** with: personality (Big Five), schedule, memory, trust, faction, secret — unlock jobs, tips, alibis, romance, betrayal |
| **Family obligations** | **Unique Identity** | **Extended family requests** (funeral contribution, school fees, medical emergency, wedding aso-ebi) — refuse → reputation hit; help → cash drain + trust gain |
| **Reputation contexts** | **Unique Identity** | **Professional / Social / Business / Criminal / Political** — each affects different doors |

### 2.7 Events & Consequences
| System | Classification | Nigeria Life Approach |
|--------|----------------|----------------------|
| Daily gem hunt, governor election | **Genre Foundation** (server events) | Keep as *live ops*; design **personal dynamic events** with preconditions, choices, persistent outcomes |
| **Dynamic life events** | **Unique Identity** | **Traffic hold-up (miss shift), task-force raid (bribe/run), POS cashback (windfall), power outage (spoilage), family emergency, scam approach, job offer, rent hike, land dispute** — each branches |
| **Investigation & law** | **Unique Identity** | **Crime path**: approach → plan → execute → evidence trail → investigation → arrest/trial/prison OR escape → life on run; **lawyer NPCs**, **bail**, **appeal** |

### 2.8 Goals & Progression
| System | Classification | Nigeria Life Approach |
|--------|----------------|----------------------|
| Lifetime dreams (5 fixed) | **Genre Foundation** | Keep as *legacy goals*; add **daily**, **short-term**, **life**, **legacy** layers — player can *create* custom goals |
| **Life missions** | **Unique Identity** | **Authored multi-step arcs**: "From Danfo to Fleet Owner", "The CcHub Exit", "Senator's Son", "Market Queen" — each with branching endings |
| **Legacy & generational** | **Unique Identity** | **Retire → play as child/protege** with inherited assets, reputation, debts; **family name** persists |

### 2.9 Locations & Travel
| System | Classification | Nigeria Life Approach |
|--------|----------------|----------------------|
| 31 Lagos venues, real hours | **Genre Foundation** | Curate **8–12 dense venues** for MVP; each with **multiple zones**, **NPC schedules**, **time-gated activities** |
| **Multi-city** | **Unique Identity** | **Lagos (commercial), Abuja (political/bureaucratic), Port Harcourt (oil/industrial), Kano (trade/agric)** — distinct job markets, costs, cultures, travel time/cost |
| **Inter-city travel** | **Opportunity** | **Flight (fast, expensive), Bus (slow, cheap, events en route), Train (mid)** — each with own minigame/events |

### 2.10 Multiplayer & Social Features
| System | Classification | Nigeria Life Approach |
|--------|----------------|----------------------|
| Shared world, live map | **Genre Foundation** | **Phase 3+**; MVP = **async multiplayer**: leaderboards, ghost data, player-run businesses visible, letters/gifts |
| **Player-to-player economy** | **Opportunity** | **Escrow, contracts, reputation-backed loans, guilds/cooperatives** — all server-validated |
| **Political gameplay** | **Unique Identity** | **Run for LGA Chairman → State Assembly → Governor → President** — policy levers affect all players in jurisdiction |

## 4. MVP Scope — What Ships First (Vertical Slice)

**Principle:** One city (Lagos), single-player + async hooks, 3 careers deep, 5 venues dense, 1 life mission authored.

| In MVP | Deferred |
|--------|----------|
| ✅ Character creation (origin combinatorial) | ❌ Multi-city (Abuja, PH, Kano) |
| ✅ 6 needs + configurable time/offline | ❌ Real-time multiplayer (shared map) |
| ✅ Cash/Bank + transaction ledger (server-authoritative) | ❌ Player-to-player transfers |
| ✅ 3 careers × 4 levels (Danfo Conductor, Junior Dev, Trader) | ❌ Full 32 careers |
| ✅ 5 venues (Home, Balogun Market, Tech Hub, Bank/POS, Nightlife) | ❌ 31 venues |
| ✅ Housing: 3 tiers (Shared, Studio, Apartment) + rent cycle | ❌ Buy-to-let, land banking |
| ✅ Skills (Driving, Coding, Communication, Hustle, Fitness) | ❌ Full 9 skills + specializations |
| ✅ 1 authored life mission ("The First Rent") + 5 dynamic events | ❌ Full mission library |
| ✅ NPC relationships (3–5 key NPCs with memory) | ❌ 100+ NPCs |
| ✅ Reputation (Professional, Social, Criminal) | ❌ Political reputation |
| ✅ Save/load + server reconciliation | ❌ Legacy/generational play |
| ✅ PWA, keyboard shortcuts, WCAG AA | ❌ In-world ads, governor election |

## 5. Competitive Moat — What's Hard to Copy

1. **Authored mission library** — content moat; requires writers, designers, QA
2. **NPC simulation with memory** — technical moat; requires AI/state architecture
3. **Interlocking reputation contexts** — design moat; balances across systems
4. **Legacy/generational progression** — retention moat; turns 1 life into many
5. **Ethical monetization + trust** — brand moat; 18+ audience pays for fairness
6. **Nigerian authenticity from systems, not skin** — cultural moat; danfo isn't a sprite, it's a mechanic (traffic, change, police, breakdown)

## 6. Risk Mitigation

| Risk | Lagos Life Precedent | Nigeria Life Mitigation |
|------|---------------------|------------------------|
| Viral growth → server crash | 100K+ concurrent caused outage | **Supabase + Edge Functions + connection pooling + load test pre-launch** |
| Content moderation | In-game chat, DMs, reports | **Phase 3 only**; MVP single-player; async = easier moderation |
| Pay-to-win perception | Game Naira top-ups | **Zero gameplay advantage from money**; cosmetics only |
| Legal/brand issues | Real brands, govt institutions in-game | **Fictionalize by default**; legal review pre-launch; Disclaimer like Lagos Life's |
| Burnout from scope | Solo dev built 32 careers in weeks | **Milestone gates**; no feature added without design doc + test plan |
