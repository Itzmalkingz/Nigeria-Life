# Nigeria Life — Brand Identity Direction

## Brand idea
**One beginning. Many choices. One life.**

## Logo concept
The chosen exploration is an abstract branching path/tree-like symbol:
- Red point: the beginning of a life.
- Branches: choices, opportunities, and possible futures.
- Blue point: the player's current choice or active path.
- Whole symbol: the life the player builds over time.

This is the current concept direction, not a claim that the logo is finalized. Use the supplied reference image if present in `references/`.

## Personality
- Confident
- Human
- Culturally grounded
- Modern
- Energetic
- Witty when appropriate
- Mature without being grim
- Ambitious without being corporate

## Suggested color palette
These are working values; don't silently change them. If contrast testing suggests a problem, report it and propose options.
- Deep Navy: `#202331` — main logo/text and strong surfaces.
- Life Red: `#F0443E` — origin point, important moments, danger/action where appropriate.
- Choice Blue: `#2764D8` — active path, selection, links, interaction.
- Warm Ivory: `#F5F1E8` — light brand background.
- Dark Background: `#11131C` — dark game interface background.
- Muted Text: `#626776` — secondary text on light surfaces.
- Optional Earth Accent: `#8A6048` — environmental/housing details, not a main logo color.
- Watermark: Deep Navy at about 8–12% opacity on light surfaces; branch symbol at about 5–8% opacity.

## Typography
- Display/headings: Sora, typically 600–800.
- Body/UI: Inter, typically 400–600.
- Major stats/numbers: Sora, typically 600–800.
- Logo lettering should be refined/customized rather than assuming a font typed as-is is the final logo.
Check actual font availability, loading behavior, and licensing before shipping.

## Background style
Use warm ivory for light brand presentations and deep dark surfaces for the game. A subtle branching-path motif may appear at low opacity. Avoid generic blobs, overused gradients, clutter, or patterns that compete with text.

## Visual exclusions
Avoid relying on the Nigerian flag, map silhouette, generic controller, palm tree, generic skyline, or random stereotypical cultural patterns as the central logo concept. Nigerian identity should emerge from the game world and brand story, not clichés.

## UI direction
The product is a responsive browser game. The in-world phone can evoke a familiar messaging interface, but do not copy WhatsApp's exact branding, logo, proprietary assets, or pixel-for-pixel UI. Use an original fictional in-game messaging brand and design language.
