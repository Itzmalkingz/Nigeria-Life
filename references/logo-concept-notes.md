# Nigeria Life Logo Reference Notes

Place the current logo exploration image in this folder and name it `nigeria-life-logo.png` (or update the filename in these notes).

Current concept:
- Abstract branching structure.
- Red origin point at the bottom.
- Dark branching paths.
- Blue point representing the current/active choice.
- Meaning: one beginning, many choices, one life.

Review criteria:
- Is the mark recognizable at small sizes?
- Does it look more like life paths than a generic circuit/network logo?
- Are the red and blue points meaningful rather than decorative?
- Can it animate or change state while retaining its core silhouette?
- Can it work in one color, on light/dark backgrounds, and without the wordmark?
- Does the wordmark remain legible at small sizes?

Instruction to AI: inspect the image if available, but do not silently redesign it. Provide observations and refinement options first.
